# R5.3 — transductors

Reopened the original scan of GOST 2.723-68, table 2 p. 10 ([scan page 14](https://meganorm.ru/Data/431/43151.pdf#page=14)). Added two DRAFT profiles: item 45 general enclosure, with two separate external conductor stubs (internal glyph not treated as a known conducting path); item 47 one-phase series transductor, two separate winding circuits and two bent core graphics. Geometry is an editorial adaptation with project dimensions and generic terminal roles, not a certified reproduction. AC/DC annotations, exact dot convention, prefix selection (currently L), and the more intricate 46, 48, 40a, 41, 42 are pending review. No magnetic simulation. Tests require physical pin clearance, independent groups and rejection of an artificial core bridge. No GUI integration.
